# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment
We are committed to providing a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone participating in this project.

## Expected Behavior
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on code, design, and behavior, not individuals.
- Assume good intent and ask for clarification before escalating.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Sharing private information without explicit consent.
- Repeated disruptive behavior after moderation requests.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation for conduct that violates this policy.

## Reporting
Open a private security/abuse report using the process in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).
